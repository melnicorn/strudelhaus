Editor autocomplete

The standard CodeMirror suggestion popup, styled to match the dark editor. A list on the left, and for the highlighted item a details box on the right with what it does and an example from the reference. Enter accepts, ↑/↓ move, Esc closes. Tab still indents. Ctrl-Space opens it on demand.

On phones (narrow editor) the details box is hidden and only the list shows.

1 · After a dot: methods

Typing .l lists matching methods. The right column is the short label. Methods you've already met in a lesson are not treated differently (keeps it simple).

2 · Inside s("…"): sound names

Only inside the strings of s() / sound(). Drum names first (with the full name), then synths. If the part has a .bank(), only that machine's sounds are listed, with their variant counts.

1setcpm(124/4)
2
3const hats = s("~ o").bank("RolandTR909")
drumohopen hi-hat · 1
oh Open hi-hat on the Roland TR-909. Add :n to pick a variant.

4 · Inside .scale("…"): scales

After the root and colon. A short list of scales that suit house and techno, each with a one-line feel. Multi-word names use colons (C:minor:pentatonic), since a space would split them into two steps.

1n("0 2 4").scale("C:").s("sawtooth")
scaleminordark, the default
scaledoriandeep house, jazzy
scalephrygiantense techno
scaleminor:pentatoniccan't go wrong
scalemajorbright, uplifting

Not shown

  • Start of an expression: s, note, n, chord, stack, arrange, cat, setcpm, signals (sine, rand…), plus your own const part names.
  • Fixed-choice arguments, e.g. .ftype(" → 12db / ladder / 24db.
  • Anywhere else inside a string (rhythms like "bd [bd bd] ~" in note(), mask(), struct()): no popup.
