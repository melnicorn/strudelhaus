Goal

Make a 909 kick play on every beat at 124 BPM.

Concept

Strudel thinks in cycles. Here, one cycle = one bar of four beats. Everything inside the quotes is squeezed into one cycle, so "bd bd bd bd" gives four evenly spaced kicks: "four on the floor".

*4 means "repeat 4 times in this step". setcpm(124/4) sets 124 beats per minute.

Task

☑ 1. Make the kick play 4 times per bar passed
☐ 2. Make it sound like a TR-909 with .bank(…)
Hint: bank names look like "RolandTR909"
✦ Challenge: write it another way that sounds identical
checks run automatically on each evaluate (queryArc predicates); "Mark done" is the manual override (D7)
